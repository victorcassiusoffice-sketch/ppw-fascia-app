# Surgery prep and recovery

PPW Protocol · v1 · Evidence B · **Doctor first**

Simple steps before and after a planned operation, led by your surgical team.

**For:** Adults with a planned (non-emergency) operation. Day 0 is the day of surgery: negative days are before, positive days after.

**Not for:** Weight-loss surgery; emergency or urgent surgery; a special diet or exercise plan set by your surgical team

**Review after:** 6 weeks · **Length:** 10 weeks

**Day 0 is the day of your operation.** Before-items count down to it; after-items start the day after.

## The plan

1. **Before: list every supplement for your team**
   09:00 · 28 days before surgery · Once · evidence C
   Write down every vitamin, herbal remedy and supplement, with doses. Bring the bottles to your pre-op visit.
2. **Before: pause supplements if told to**
   09:00 · 14 days before surgery · Once · evidence C
   Your team may ask you to stop supplements at least 2 weeks before surgery. Follow their plan.
3. **Before: daily walk** (30 min)
   10:00 · from 28 days before surgery · Every day · evidence B
   Stay active until surgery if your team agrees. In trials, mostly of bowel surgery, prehab programmes shortened hospital stays.
4. **Before: smoke-free day (if you smoke)**
   On waking · from 28 days before surgery · Every day · evidence B
   Stopping smoking before surgery lowers the risk of problems afterwards. Ask your GP or pharmacist for support.
5. **Before: alcohol-free day (if you drink a lot)**
   60 min before dinner · from 28 days before surgery · Every day · evidence B
   More than 3 drinks a day? Get your doctor's help to stop. You may need medicine for withdrawal.
6. **After: short walks as allowed** (5 min)
   11:00 · from 1 days after surgery · Every day · evidence B
   Get up and walk as soon as your team says it is safe. Add a little more each day.
7. **After: breathing exercises from your team** (5 min)
   09:30 · from 1 days after surgery · Every day · evidence B
   If you are shown breathing or physio exercises, do them as taught. They can cut chest problems after tummy surgery.
8. **After: protein at every meal**
   At lunch · from 1 days after surgery · Every day · evidence C
   Eat as soon as your team allows. Aim for about 1.2-1.5 g protein per kg body weight a day unless told otherwise. Kidney disease: ask first.
9. **After: restart supplements only with an OK**
   09:00 · 1 days after surgery · Once · evidence C
   Ask before restarting supplements. Don't add new 'healing' mixes: most have no proof of benefit.

## Safety

- Your surgical team's advice comes first. This plan does not replace it.
- Never stop prescribed medicines, including blood thinners, unless your team tells you to.
- Often paused before surgery: ginkgo, garlic, ginseng, ginger, high-dose turmeric, feverfew, saw palmetto and others. How long depends on the supplement (from a day to 2 weeks). Your team will tell you.
- Kava, valerian and St John's wort can affect anaesthesia. Tell your anaesthetist.
- Fish oil: a large heart-surgery trial found no extra bleeding. Ask your team whether to continue.
- Heavy drinker? Don't stop suddenly on your own. Ask your doctor for help.
- After surgery, get urgent help for a hot, red, swollen or painful leg, chest pain or sudden breathlessness.
- Kidney disease: ask before raising your protein.
- Follow your team's advice on wound care, lifting and driving.

## Key studies

- Preoperative Management of Surgical Patients Using Dietary Supplements: SPAQI Consensus Statement (2021, guideline). Ask every patient about supplements. Without proven benefit, there is little downside to pausing them before surgery. [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33741131/)
- ESPEN guideline on clinical nutrition in surgery - Update 2025 (2025, guideline). Avoid long fasting, eat again early after surgery, and get moving early to help protein use and muscle. [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40957230/)
- Effects of Nutritional Prehabilitation, With and Without Exercise, on Outcomes of Patients Who Undergo Colorectal Surgery (2018, meta-analysis). Prehab before bowel surgery cut hospital stay by about 2 days. [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29750973/)
- Preoperative smoking cessation interventions and their effects on smoking abstinence and postoperative outcomes (2025, meta-analysis). Help to stop smoking before surgery probably cut complications by about 27%. [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40954410/)
- Perioperative alcohol cessation intervention for postoperative complications (2018, Cochrane review). Intensive help to stop drinking 4-8 weeks before surgery probably reduced complications (3 small trials). [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30408162/)
- Fish Oil and Perioperative Bleeding (2018, RCT). In 1,516 heart-surgery patients, fish oil did not increase bleeding and fewer transfusions were needed. [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30571332/)
- Non-drug perioperative interventions to reduce postoperative pulmonary complications after abdominal surgery (2026, meta-analysis). 255 trials: physiotherapy and early mobilisation were among measures that helped prevent chest complications. [Link](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41956522/)
